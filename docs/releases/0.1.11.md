## English

### Highlights

- **Xiaomi and Ninebot scooters.** New support for Xiaomi M365-family and Ninebot retail scooters: scanning, an encrypted
  connection, registration with an explicit consent screen, a dashboard, and relaying the scooter to the AR glasses' HUD.
- **Pairing keys stay yours.** A scooter's pairing key is stored encrypted on the phone (AES-256-GCM, non-exportable Android
  Keystore key). You can delete one scooter's key, type a key in by hand, and back keys up to a passphrase-encrypted `.rfbond`
  file that needs your fingerprint or screen lock before it is written.
- **A steadier HUD link.** The bridge rebuilds its pipeline after an unexpected failure instead of idling, a hung reconnect is
  abandoned after 4 s instead of 10 s, and the HUD is shown again whenever the glasses connect afresh.
- **A diagnostic log you can save.** Settings -> "Diagnostic log" saves recent bridge and glasses events (no keys or tokens,
  Bluetooth addresses shortened) so a problem can be explained afterwards.

### New

- Xiaomi M365 / Ninebot scooter support, including lock and unlock (see "Not verified on hardware").
- Read-only telemetry for Nosfet wheels (Apex, Aero and the older LeaperKim models) with the vendor's own battery curves.
- Per-app language picker and per-glasses HUD layout profiles, synced to the glasses.
- The scanner can change a device's brand on any card and offers Inmotion I1 / I2 and Ninebot N1 / N2 separately. A brand you
  pick for a device the app cannot recognise is remembered until the app restarts.
- A website in 18 languages and a README split into a front page and a reference (docs).

### Fixed

- **HUD bridge with a scooter.** Sending an M365 to the HUD used to fail silently and left the glasses on "phone connected"
  with no data.
- **Speed.** A Xiaomi scooter that creeps backwards or jitters at standstill no longer shows 60+ km/h.
- **Inmotion V11 / I2.** A frame split right after an escape byte is no longer dropped; the V11 early layout is only used on
  firmware older than 1.4.
- **KingSong F18.** `KS-F18`, `KS_F18`, `KSF18` and `KS F18` are recognised; frames that cannot be decoded now leave a
  diagnostic instead of silence.
- **Safety.** Vehicle actions (beep, lights, speed limit, power off, ...) now require a known speed of 0. A device with an
  unrecognised name is no longer guessed to be a Begode wheel: you choose the brand.
- **Privacy.** The glasses' pairing token was written to the log at INFO level; it no longer is.
- **HUD blank.** A HUD blanked with the volume keys or the ring no longer stays blank, with nothing to explain it, after the
  glasses or the bridge restart.

### Privacy

The privacy policy (effective 2026-10-08) now describes scooter pairing keys, the `.rfbond` backup and the on-device diagnostic
log. RideFlux still has no internet permission and sends nothing anywhere.

### Not verified on hardware

Tested with the Begode A2, a Xiaomi M365 (connection, telemetry, HUD relay) and Rokid glasses. **Not** verified on a real
vehicle: scooter lock / unlock and registration, KingSong F18, Inmotion V11 and Nosfet. They are implemented against published
protocol data and unit tests; please report what you see.

### Upgrading

Install over the previous version; the signing certificate is unchanged and your data is kept. The HUD bridge is still off by
default and has to be switched on again after an update or after the system clears the app.

## 繁體中文

### 重點

- **小米與 Ninebot 滑板車。** 新增小米 M365 系列與 Ninebot 零售滑板車支援：掃描、加密連線、需要您明確同意的註冊畫面、儀表板，
  以及把滑板車轉送到 AR 眼鏡的 HUD。
- **配對金鑰由您掌握。** 滑板車的配對金鑰以加密方式存在手機上（AES-256-GCM，Android Keystore 中不可匯出的金鑰）。您可以刪除單一
  滑板車的金鑰、手動輸入金鑰，並備份成以密語加密的 `.rfbond` 檔；寫出檔案前需要先以指紋或螢幕鎖確認。
- **更穩定的 HUD 連線。** 橋接在意外失敗後會重建管線而不是停擺；卡住的重新連線改為 4 秒就放棄重試（原本 10 秒）；眼鏡每次重新連線
  都會把 HUD 設回顯示。
- **可儲存的診斷日誌。** 設定頁「診斷日誌」可儲存最近的橋接與眼鏡事件（不含金鑰或權杖，藍牙位址已縮短），出問題後才有辦法說明原因。

### 新增

- 小米 M365／Ninebot 滑板車支援，含鎖車與解鎖（見「尚未在實車驗證」）。
- Nosfet 電動獨輪車唯讀遙測（Apex、Aero 與較舊的 LeaperKim 機型），使用原廠的電量曲線。
- 每個 App 的語言選擇，以及每副眼鏡各自的 HUD 版面設定，並同步到眼鏡。
- 掃描頁每張卡片都能更改品牌，並分開提供 Inmotion I1／I2 與 Ninebot N1／N2。您為無法辨識的裝置選的品牌，在 App 重新啟動前會被記住。
- 18 種語言的網站，以及拆成首頁與參考文件的 README。

### 修正

- **滑板車送到 HUD 橋接。** 以前把 M365 送到 HUD 會無聲失敗，眼鏡停在「手機已連線」而沒有資料。
- **時速。** 小米滑板車倒退或靜止時的抖動，不會再顯示 60 km/h 以上。
- **Inmotion V11／I2。** 在跳脫字元後被切開的封包不再被丟棄；V11 早期版面只用在 1.4 以前的韌體。
- **KingSong F18。** 可辨識 `KS-F18`、`KS_F18`、`KSF18`、`KS F18`；無法解碼的封包現在會留下診斷，而不是毫無動靜。
- **安全。** 車輛動作（嗶聲、燈光、速度上限、關機等）現在要求速度已知且為 0。名稱無法辨識的裝置不再被猜成 Begode，改由您選擇品牌。
- **隱私。** 眼鏡的配對權杖原本以 INFO 層級寫進日誌，現在不會了。
- **HUD 黑屏。** 用音量鍵或指環隱藏的 HUD，在眼鏡或橋接重新啟動後不會再無緣無故一直黑著。

### 隱私

隱私權政策（2026-10-08 生效）新增說明滑板車配對金鑰、`.rfbond` 備份與裝置上的診斷日誌。RideFlux 仍然沒有網路權限，也不會把任何資料傳到任何地方。

### 尚未在實車驗證

已用 Begode A2、小米 M365（連線、遙測、HUD 轉送）與 Rokid 眼鏡測試。**尚未**在實車驗證：滑板車鎖車／解鎖與註冊、KingSong F18、Inmotion V11、Nosfet。
這些是依公開的協定資料與單元測試實作的，歡迎回報您的使用結果。

### 升級

直接覆蓋安裝；簽章憑證不變，資料會保留。HUD 橋接預設仍是關閉，更新後或系統清除 App 後需要重新打開。
